# Excel Analysis

This repository contains a comprehensive financial analysis workbook developed in Excel to evaluate business performance, assess financial health, and support planning and decision-making.

## Project Overview

This project is a financial analysis and planning workbook developed in Microsoft Excel to evaluate business performance, assess financial health, and support strategic decision-making. The model brings together key financial statements and planning tools to provide a practical view of how a business generates value, manages resources, and forecasts future outcomes.

The workbook is designed to help stakeholders analyze revenue and profitability trends, review asset and liability composition, assess cash generation and usage, and model short-term liquidity needs. It serves as an example of how Excel can be used to create structured financial reporting and scenario-based planning tools for business analysis.

## Tools Used

- Microsoft Excel
- Excel formulas and financial functions
- Pivot tables and tabular analysis
- Charts and dashboards for financial reporting
- Scenario planning and forecasting models
- Cash flow and budgeting templates
- Formatting, conditional logic, and model organization for clarity

## Key Findings

- The workbook highlights how revenue, costs, and profitability interact across the income statement.
- It provides a clear view of the company's balance sheet structure, including asset allocation and financing mix.
- Cash flow analysis helps identify how money moves through the business and where liquidity pressure may arise.
- Forecasting sections support planning for future growth, expenses, and working capital needs.
- The cash budget emphasizes the importance of monitoring inflows and outflows to maintain financial stability.
- Overall, the model demonstrates a strong use of Excel for business finance, planning, and performance evaluation.

## Included Analysis

The project includes the following financial components:

- Income Statement
- Balance Sheet
- Statement of Cash Flows
- Financial Forecast
- Cash Budget

## Business Impact

This project showcases practical financial analysis skills and highlights the value of structured data-driven decision-making. It is particularly relevant for roles involving finance, budgeting, reporting, and strategic planning, where accurate analysis and clear presentation of financial health are essential.
